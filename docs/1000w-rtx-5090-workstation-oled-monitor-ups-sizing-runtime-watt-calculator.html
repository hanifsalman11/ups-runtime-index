Why is 2,200 VA only rated for 1,980 Watts on the 1000W RTX 5090 Workstation + OLED Monitor UPS Sizing?

VA (Volt-Amps) measures Apparent Power, while Watts measures Real Power. The ratio between them is the Power Factor ($ ext{PF} = 1980 \div 2200 = 0.90$). You must never exceed EITHER the 2,200 VA ceiling or the 1,980 Watt ceiling.
