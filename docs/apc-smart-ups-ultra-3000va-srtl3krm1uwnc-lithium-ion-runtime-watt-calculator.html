Frequently Asked Questions (APC Smart-UPS Ultra 3000VA SRTL3KRM1UWNC Lithium-Ion)

How long will the APC Smart-UPS Ultra 3000VA SRTL3KRM1UWNC Lithium-Ion run my PC, NAS, or network rack?

With 600 VAh of battery capacity (48V LiFePO4 / Li-Ion Pack), runtime scales non-linearly by Peukert's Law: ~6 minutes at 100% load (3000W), ~15 minutes at 50% load (1500W), ~34 minutes at 25% load (750W), and over 400 minutes for a 65W Starlink + Wi-Fi router load.

Why is 3,000 VA only rated for 3,000 Watts on the APC Smart-UPS Ultra 3000VA SRTL3KRM1UWNC Lithium-Ion?

VA (Volt-Amps) measures Apparent Power, while Watts measures Real Power. The ratio between them is the Power Factor ($ ext{PF} = 3000 \div 3000 = 1.00$). You must never exceed EITHER the 3,000 VA ceiling or the 3,000 Watt ceiling.

What replacement battery does the APC Smart-UPS Ultra 3000VA SRTL3KRM1UWNC Lithium-Ion take?

It uses 48V LiFePO4 / Li-Ion Pack high-rate discharge Sealed Lead-Acid (SLA) batteries with 0.250-inch (F2) quick-disconnect spade terminals (totaling 600 VAh). You can replace them every 3–4 years in under 10 minutes using your existing wiring harness.
