Frequently Asked Questions (Apc Smart Ups 750 Battery Replacement)

How long will the Apc Smart Ups 750 Battery Replacement run my gaming PC or NAS?

At 100% capacity (1010W), runtime is approximately 7 minutes, scaling to 16 minutes at 50% load (505W), and 37 minutes at 25% load (252W). For low-power network infrastructure such as a typical 65W NAS and router bundle, expected backup runtime is roughly 95 to 110 minutes depending on battery cell age and ambient temperature.

What is the difference between 1500 VA and 1010 Watts?

Volt-Amperes (VA) represent Apparent Power (RMS voltage multiplied by RMS current), whereas True Watts (W) represent real usable power consumed by computer circuitry. The ratio between them is the 0.67 Power Factor (PF = W / VA); crossing either the 1500VA ceiling or the 1010W thermal threshold will trigger an immediate inverter overload protection fault.

What replacement battery cartridge fits the Apc Smart Ups 750 Battery Replacement?

The unit requires a 24V DC bus configuration composed of two 12V 9Ah high-rate discharge batteries wired in series (totaling 216 VAh) with standard F2 (0.250-inch) spade terminals.
