Frequently Asked Questions (Apc Smart Ups 1500)

How long will the Apc Smart Ups 1500 run my PC, NAS, or network rack?

With 216 VAh of battery capacity (2x 12V 9Ah SLA/LiFePO4), runtime scales non-linearly by Peukert's Law: ~7 minutes at 100% load (1010W), ~16 minutes at 50% load (500W), ~37 minutes at 25% load (250W), and over 144 minutes for a 65W Starlink + Wi-Fi router load.

Why is 1,500 VA only rated for 1,010 Watts on the Apc Smart Ups 1500?

VA (Volt-Amps) measures Apparent Power, while Watts measures Real Power. The ratio between them is the Power Factor ($ ext{PF} = 1010 \div 1500 = 0.67$). You must never exceed EITHER the 1,500 VA ceiling or the 1,010 Watt ceiling.

What replacement battery does the Apc Smart Ups 1500 take?

It uses 2x 12V 9Ah SLA/LiFePO4 high-rate discharge Sealed Lead-Acid (SLA) batteries with 0.250-inch (F2) quick-disconnect spade terminals (totaling 216 VAh). You can replace them every 3–4 years in under 10 minutes using your existing wiring harness.
